## Dog Object Permanence

I think there's a lot of variation in how animals deal with objects. In this case, the dog clearly knows that the ball _exists_, but cannot fathom where it went to. So it understands object permanence in the sense that it knows the ball still exists, but cannot understand how it disappeared from its field of view.
